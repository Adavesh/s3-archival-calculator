Assumptions and limitations

Each archived object incurs 8 KiB of metadata billed at Standard rates and 32 KiB billed at Deep Archive rates. Transition fees are charged per object. The initial hot-storage period is excluded from both alternatives. Reference rates are illustrative US East pricing inputs, not a live pricing feed; replace them with your region's applicable rates. Retrieval, restore, temporary copies, taxes and other request charges are excluded. Deep Archive has a 180-day minimum storage duration; this calculator assumes at least six months of archival retention. S3 lifecycle defaults exclude objects under 128 KiB unless overridden. The model assumes equal-sized objects; use separate calculations for size cohorts when evaluating a mixed workload.

Reference: AWS S3 pricing. All calculations use binary size units (1 GiB = 1,048,576 KiB). No information is transmitted by this calculator.
